This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models covering multiple disciplines and difficulty levels. The benchmark provides a standardized way to evaluate the capabilities of large language models in Chinese, spanning from middle school to graduate-level knowledge across various domains.
