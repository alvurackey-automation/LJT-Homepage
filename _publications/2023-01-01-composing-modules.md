This paper explores composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations, enabling more flexible and efficient model adaptation. The work demonstrates that arithmetic operations on parameter-efficient modules (such as LoRA) can effectively combine and transfer knowledge across tasks, providing a more efficient alternative to full model fine-tuning.
